3D Printing & Fabrication

The Recessed DeWalt Bucket

Model an open rectangular box in AutoCAD. The inside cavity must snugly fit your DeWalt battery dock. Give the bucket a 0.5" outer flange. Cut a hole in the right-side wood panel, drop this bucket in, and screw the flange to the outside wood.

Tractrix Aero-Ports (Ender 3 V3 SE / Bambu Setup)

Feed PETG filament directly from a dryer box to ensure zero stringing. Slice in OrcaSlicer with 5 wall loops and a 0.16mm layer height. Print flat on the outer flange.

Z = 0.00" (Outer Face): Inner Rad = 1.25", Outer Rad = 1.625" Z = 0.18" (Flange): Extrude a 3.25" dia. mounting flange Z = 0.70" (Start Throat): Inner Rad = 0.75" (1.5" ID) Z = 3.50" (End Throat): Inner Rad = 0.75" Z = 4.20" (Inner Flare): Inner Rad = 1.25", Outer Rad = 1.375"

The Dummy-Proof Build Protocol

Phase 1: Structure & Support

1. Pre-drill and assemble the Top, Bottom, Sides, and Solid Center Divider using wood glue and screws. Do NOT attach the Front or Back panels yet.
2. Install the hollow Window-Pane Braces inside each chamber, exactly halfway between the center divider and the outer side walls. Glue securely.
3. Cut the rectangular hole in the Right Side panel and mount your Recessed DeWalt Bucket.
4. Recess the 3/8"-16 tripod threaded insert into the dead center of the exterior Bottom panel.

Phase 2: The Acoustic Treatment

1. Run a heavy bead of silicone caulk along every single interior wood joint.
2. Press patches of Butyl sound deadener firmly onto the interior Top, Bottom, and Side panels.
3. Weigh out roughly 3.0 ounces of Dacron polyfill per chamber. Loosely fluff it and place it in the back half of the chambers.

Phase 3: The Power Routing

1. Mount the XH-M609 Cutoff, the KABT-250 Amp, and the DPDT toggle switch to the inside Back Panel.
2. Wire the DeWalt dock (+) and (-) to the XH-M609 IN terminals. Program the screen to cut off at 15.2V.
3. Wire XH-M609 OUT terminals to Position A on the DPDT switch.
4. Wire the 24V Wall Jack to Position B on the DPDT switch.
5. Wire the DPDT Center Output Terminals to the KABT-250 power input and the LED Voltmeter.
6. Run 14 AWG speaker wires from the Amp to the front speaker cavities.

Phase 4: Final Button-Up

1. Glue and screw the Front Baffle and Back Panel onto the main shell.
2. Apply foam weatherstripping tape around the lips of the speaker and port cutouts.
3. Connect the speaker wires to the Pioneer 6x9s, drop them in, and screw down tight.
4. Drop the 3D printed Aero-Ports into their holes and screw them down tight.

SigmaStudio DSP Tuning

TWS (True Wireless Stereo) Pairing

Power on both boomboxes. Press and hold the "Pair" button on the primary unit's control panel until they sync. Your phone connects to the primary unit, and the secondary unit automatically plays synchronized stereo audio up to 30 feet away.

Flashing the DSP Hardware

Plug the 6-pin cable from the Dayton KPX programmer into the KABT-250 amplifier. Plug the KPX into your Windows PC. Turn the boombox power ON, and open Analog Devices SigmaStudio.

The Backyard Tune (Open Air)

Open air swallows bass. In SigmaStudio, set up this block chain:

  • Protection Filter: 4th-Order High-Pass Butterworth at 42 Hz.
  • Sub-Bass Boost: Low-Shelf Filter at 60 Hz (+4.0 dB).
  • Vocal Smoothing: Parametric EQ dip at 3.2 kHz (-3.0 dB, Q = 1.4).
  • Treble Roll-off: High-Shelf cut starting at 6 kHz (-3 dB).
The Garage Tune (Enclosed Space)

The hard concrete floor will create a massive low-end echo. Save a second EQ preset in SigmaStudio:

  • Bass Reduction: Change the Low-Shelf to a -2.0 dB cut at 80 Hz to prevent muddy booming.
To save the tune permanently to the board, click the "Link Compile Download" button, then right-click the ADAU1701 block in the hardware tab and select "Write Latest Compilation to E2PROM".
