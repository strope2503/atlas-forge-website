Product Overview & What It Does

Atlas Scout Pro V8.0 is a rugged, all-weather portable camera station housed inside a rolling hard case with an elevated 20ft air-cushioned mast. It captures the entire soccer field using a 3-camera 4K array, instantly processing live video on an onboard NVIDIA supercomputer. It broadcasts crystal-clear 1080p live streams and tactical analytics directly to parent smartphones and coach tablets over local cellular or Wi-Fi hotspots.

Core Features & AI Analytics Capabilities

  • 180° Panoramic Field Capture: Utilizes 3x Reolink CX810 4K cameras with F1.0 super-aperture lenses to stitch together a flawless, distortion-free view of the entire pitch.
  • The Calibration Stick (Corner-Flag Homography Grid): A physical field tool planted at key pitch reference points during setup. The onboard computer instantly detects it to map camera pixels into precise 2D metric coordinates (105m x 68m) in under 3 seconds.
  • Autonomous Player Tracking (BoT-SORT ReID): Automatically locks onto player jerseys and maintains persistent IDs across overlapping camera angles so you never lose track of your athlete.
  • 3D Skeletal Biomechanics & ACL Fatigue Alerts: Extracts 17 joint keypoints per player in real-time, calculating knee flexion angles and flagging high-risk deceleration G-force spikes before injuries happen.
  • Kinetic-Entropy VFR (Variable Framerate): Automatically scales recording speed from 1 FPS during dead balls up to 60 FPS during fast breakaways, slashing video storage footprints by 99% (under 50MB per 90-minute match).
  • Rugged Power Plant: Powered by a Newport 12V 50Ah AGM marine battery providing 8 to 10+ hours of continuous game recording with active cooling and internal fuse protection.
