Product Overview & What It Does

Atlas Scout Pro V8.0 is an all-weather portable sideline station housed in The Apache Vault™ (Harbor Freight Apache 5800) with The Sentinel Mast™ (20ft air-cushioned mast). It captures the pitch via The Trident Array™ (3x Reolink CX810 4K cameras), powered by The Apex Core™ (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano). It streams live 1080p video and spatial analytics directly to parent smartphones and coach tablets with zero cloud latency.

Core Hardware & AI Codenames

  • The Trident Array™ (180° Panoramic Optics): 3x 4K cameras with F1.0 super-aperture lenses stitching a seamless 9K field view.
  • The Calibration Stick™ (Corner-Flag Homography Grid): Physical field tool used at pitch reference points. The AI detects it in under 3 seconds to lock camera pixels to exact 2D metric pitch coordinates ($105\text{m} \times 68\text{m}$).
  • BoT-SORT ReID™ (Autonomous Player Lock): Visual embedding AI that locks onto player jerseys and maintains persistent IDs across overlapping camera angles.
  • 3D Skeletal ACL Safety™ (Biomechanical Alerts): Real-time extraction of 17 joint keypoints per player, monitoring knee flexion angles and flagging high-risk deceleration G-force spikes.
  • Kinetic-Entropy VFR™ (Dynamic Framerate): Scales recording speeds dynamically (1 FPS dead balls $\rightarrow$ 60 FPS breakaways), compressing a 90-minute match footprint under 50 MB using the SVG-C™ Codec.
  • The Forge Reactor™ (10+ Hour Power Core): Newport 12V 50Ah AGM marine battery managed by The Power Grid Node™ (19V DC Booster & Fuse Block) and cooled via The Thermal Chimney™.
