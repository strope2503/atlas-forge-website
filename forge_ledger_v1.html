Product Overview & What It Does

The Forge Ledger is a cross-platform financial management application built using React Native (Expo) and a robust local API backend. It connects directly to bank accounts to automate transaction categorization, track cash flow, and separate personal versus business expenses. Designed to integrate seamlessly with the Atlas Forge ecosystem, it provides sports club administrators and business owners with crystal-clear fiscal visibility and audit-ready bookkeeping.

Core Features & Capabilities

  • Live Bank Integration: Securely syncs transaction feeds across multiple business and personal accounts in real-time.
  • Automated Tax Categorization: Instantly sorts expenditures to maximize business deductions and streamline year-end tax preparation.
  • Sports Club Accounting: Custom ledger views tailored specifically for youth sports team budgeting, tournament registration fees, equipment purchasing, and sponsor tracking.
  • Local-First Security: High-performance local database caching backed by encrypted storage to protect sensitive financial records.
