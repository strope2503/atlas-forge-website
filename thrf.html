THE RESEARCH QUESTION

What changes when video is not treated as a flat pixel grid?

THRF explores whether media can be represented and composed through mathematical fields that are evaluated at display time, potentially changing storage, seek behavior, compositing, and spatial projection[cite: 11].

Representation

Wave harmonics in a non-Euclidean phase space[cite: 11].

Evaluation

Closed-form GPU compute at render time[cite: 11].

Composition

Field superposition instead of conventional pixel blending[cite: 11].

Spatiality

Native ambitions for 6DoF and immersive display[cite: 11].
