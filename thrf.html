THRF™—Topological Holographic Resonant Fields—is an experimental codec and compositing architecture investigating wave-based media representation, spatial rendering, and radically different performance characteristics.

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

What changes when video is not treated as a flat pixel grid?

THRF explores whether media can be represented and composed through mathematical fields that are evaluated at display time, potentially changing storage, seek behavior, compositing, and spatial projection.

Representation

Wave harmonics in a non-Euclidean phase space.

Evaluation

Closed-form GPU compute at render time.

Composition

Field superposition instead of conventional pixel blending.

Spatiality

Native ambitions for 6DoF and immersive display.
