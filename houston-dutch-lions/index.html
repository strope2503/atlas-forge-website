Tactical Edge
🚩 Attack Corner: Near Post Flick Primary

LCB / #4: Sprints across near post for flick-on.
ST / #9 & RCB / #5: Overload 6-yd box & back post.
CDM / #6: Sits edge of D to volley clearances.
LB & RB: Hold halfway line (Rest Defense).

🚩 Attack Corner: 2v1 Short Overload Trick

CAM / #10: Runs short to create 2v1 with taker.
Inswing Cross: Driven into the corridor between keeper & 6-yd box for crashing runners.

🛡️ Defending Corners: Zonal + 2 CDMs Defensive

4 Zonal Line: Near post (FB), central 6-yd line (CB1, CB2, FB).
2 CDMs (#6 & #8): Man-mark opponent's best aerial jumpers.
ST (#9): Sits on midfield circle ready for counter-attack.

🎯 Direct Free Kicks & Wall Setup Dead Ball

Central Wall: 4 players tight (tallest on outside).
Angle Kicks: 2-3 player wall covering near post; GK commands far side.
