Official 4-2-3-1 Set Piece Playbook

🎯 Corner A: Near-Post Flick ATTACK

CB1 sprints to near post to glance flick. ST (#9) & CB2 crash far post for the tap-in. #6 CDM holds edge of 18-yard box for second balls. Timing is everything: start run as kicker steps to the ball.
🎥 Watch: Near Post Corner Mastery

⚽ Corner B: 2v1 Short Overload ATTACK

CAM (#10) checks short to corner taker for 2v1 overload. Pulls defender out, opening angle for driven cutback to #8 CDM arriving at the penalty spot. Winger clears out space at the edge.
🎥 Watch: Unstoppable Short Corners

🛡️ Defending Corners (Hybrid) DEFENSE

4-man zonal line across 6-yard box (LB, CB1, CB2, RB). #6 & #8 CDMs man-mark opponent runners. ST (#9) stays on halfway line for instant counter-attack outlet. Focus on attacking the ball, not just standing in zones.
🎥 Watch: Hybrid Corner Defense

👟 Direct Free Kicks & Walls ORGANIZATION

GK calls wall count (3-4 players). Tallest player on near post side of wall. #6 CDM guards wall-end peel off. Wingers drop to edge of box to sweep rebounds. Do not turn your back to the ball when in the wall!
🎥 Watch: Building a Solid Wall
