CoreGrid™ Engine

The high-performance distributed computing architecture powering the entire Atlas Forge product ecosystem.

Product Overview & What It Does

CoreGrid™ is a UDP-based mesh networking and zero-decompression database engine. It links standard PCs, edge GPUs (like the NVIDIA Jetson), and mobile phone NPUs (Neural Processing Units) into a unified decentralized supercomputer. By slicing heavy data workloads locally and streaming lightweight spatial vector graphs (`.svgc`), CoreGrid™ enables real-time AI processing and instant video analysis without relying on expensive cloud servers.

Core Features & Capabilities

  • Zero-Decompression Querying: Evaluates database searches directly against compressed spatial manifolds without uncompressing raw bytes.
  • Mesh Worker Slicing: Dynamically distributes heavy computer vision and AI rendering tasks across connected edge nodes.
  • Sub-Millisecond IPC: Ultra-fast local socket communication designed for real-time video editing, audio synthesis, and sports telemetry.
